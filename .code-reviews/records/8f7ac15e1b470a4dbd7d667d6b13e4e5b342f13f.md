## Summary

Adds commit validation to the ticketboard store: a new `Board.checkCommit()` that `updateTask` (when `commit` is patched) and `recordCommit` now call. It requires the sha to exist, be on the ticket's branch, not already be on the base branch, and not already be recorded for another task. The `build` SKILL.md is updated to require a review before every commit, to treat a hook-blocked commit as "not committed", and to record `git rev-parse HEAD` taken right after a successful commit. This is a re-review: the three earlier findings were re-evaluated against the changed lines. No stack marker matched (no package.json etc.), so a generic review was applied.
